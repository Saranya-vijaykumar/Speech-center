Our Purpose & Mission

Why SpeakBloom Was Built: Creating a Calmer, More Effective Speech Clinic

SpeakBloom Center was established in 2018 to solve a common clinical problem: traditional hospital and school therapy rooms are often sterile, hurried, and sensorially overwhelming. Children felt anxious, and parents were left out of the session.

Our center was engineered from the ground up as a botanical, sensory-regulated healing space. Here, speech therapy combines clinical rigor with joyful, child-led play, while giving caregivers direct tools to extend communicative milestones right at home.

Naturalistic Play Therapy

Embedded speech targets in engaging games and developmental play.

Integrated Parent Coaching

Caregivers observe and learn micro-strategies for mealtime and home routines.

Neuro-Affirming Care

Respecting authentic autistic communication and Gestalt language acquisition.

Objective Diagnostic Tracking

Standardized CELF-5, GFTA-3, and PLS-5 milestone progress measurement.

Accreditations

Certified Clinical Credentials You Can Rely On

Every clinician at SpeakBloom Center maintains active board certifications, ongoing clinical research education, and adherence to strict medical ethics.

ASHA CCC-SLP Board

All staff clinicians hold active Certificates of Clinical Competence from the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association.

PROMPT Certified

Tactile-kinesthetic oral motor techniques specifically engineered for Childhood Apraxia of Speech and motor speech disorders.

NLA / Gestalt Trained

Specialized Natural Language Acquisition protocols for autistic children who communicate through scripting and echolalia.

HIPAA & Gold Standard

Standardized CELF-5, GFTA-3, and PLS-5 diagnostic testing batteries with 100% encrypted HIPAA medical data compliance.
