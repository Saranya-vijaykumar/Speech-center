INFANT & TODDLER EARLY COMMUNICATION

0 – 2 Years: Babbling, First Words & Gestures

The first 24 months lay the critical foundation for all future expressive speech. From reflexive cooing to intentional pointing and first words, early responsive interactions shape neural connections.

Meet Dr. Ananya (Toddler Specialist) →
6–12 Months

Babbling & Joint Attention

  • Canonical babbling ("ba-ba", "ma-ma")
  • Responds to own name with head turn
  • Early non-verbal gestures (waving, reaching)
12–18 Months

First Spoken Words & Pointing

  • 10 to 20 functional single words
  • Index finger pointing to request toys
  • Imitating animal noises ("moo", "beep")
18–24 Months

Vocabulary Explosion

  • 50+ spoken distinct words
  • Spontaneous 2-word combinations ("more milk")
  • Understood by family caregivers ~50% of time
