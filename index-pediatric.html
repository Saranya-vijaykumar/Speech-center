How your child experiences SpeakBloom ♥

Our 4-Stage Sensory Play Process

No white coats, no rigid testing tables. Here is what happens from your child's first step into our center.

01

Gentle Play Evaluation

Our clinician observes your child interacting naturally with bubbles, puzzles, and sensory bins while conversing with parents.

02

Tailored Sensory Roadmap

We map specific phonetic targets, oral motor goals, and emotional regulation strategies into playful weekly milestones.

03

Joyful Playroom Sessions

45-minute interactive sessions where speech targets are seamlessly embedded into swings, puppet dialogues, and tactile art.

04

Parent Play Home Toolkit

Parents receive video clips, printable flash games, and daily routine prompts to reinforce new sounds during bath, bedtime, and meals.

Explore Speech Sounds By Developmental Age

Tap on each phoneme sound below to discover when children typically master it and try our clinician-approved mirror cues!

/b/ Mastered by: 24 Months "The Popping Bubble Sound"

How to Practice the /b/ Sound with Your Child:

Have your child press both lips gently together like holding a secret, then pop them open with a puff of air while feeling their voicebox vibrate ("b-b-bubble!").

Try These Fun Practice Words:

Ball, Bear, Boat, Big, Bye-bye, Banana
