Evidence-Based Clinical Protocol

Childhood Apraxia of Speech is a motor planning disorder where the brain has difficulty planning the precise muscle sequences for speech. We utilize DTTC (Dynamic Temporal and Tactile Cueing) and PROMPT therapy to build fluid motor memories.

Diagnostic Checklist

When Is It Time for an Evaluation?

Common indicators that suggest a specialized speech & language evaluation will be beneficial.

01

Inconsistent Speech Sound Errors

Saying a word correctly once, then saying it completely differently on the next try.

02

Groping Movements of the Mouth

Visible struggle or silent searching movements with lips and tongue when trying to speak.

03

Vowel Distortions

Inability to produce pure vowel sounds (e.g. substituting /oo/ for /ah/).

04

Loss of Clarity as Words Grow Longer

Can say simple words ("go"), but multisyllabic words ("banana") become unintelligible.

Step-by-Step Care Journey

Your Treatment Roadmap

From initial diagnostic assessment to conversational mastery and discharge.

01

Motor Speech Diagnostic (DEMSS)

Differential diagnosis distinguishing motor apraxia from phonological delay or dysarthria.

02

Core Functional Vocabulary Core

Select 5-10 high-impact words essential for the child's daily autonomy and emotional connection.

03

High-Intensity DTTC Motor Practice

Perform 100+ daily motor repetitions with simultaneous cueing, slow rate, and tactile fading.

04

Prosodic Natural Flow

Integrate natural speech rhythm, stress, and melodic sentence intonation.
