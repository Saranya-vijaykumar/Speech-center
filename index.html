Every word begins with connection.

When a child struggles to express their thoughts—whether facing late speech milestones, unclear pronunciation, or stuttering blocks—it can feel overwhelming for the entire family.

At SpeakBloom, we replace frustration with playful discovery. Through structured play therapy, phonics games, and personalized parent coaching, we nurture each child's natural desire to speak and connect.

Speech therapy is a safe, encouraging space where children overcome communication barriers, practice target sounds, and develop lasting conversational pride.

Ready to take the first step? Let's begin your child's communication journey.

Hi, I'm Dr. Elena.

I am a Licensed Speech-Language Pathologist (CCC-SLP) and Clinical Director at SpeakBloom. With over 14 years of clinical experience, I specialize in toddler speech delays, stuttering modification, childhood apraxia, and neurodiversity-affirming AAC communication.

My philosophy centers on creating joyful, stress-free clinical experiences that empower parents with actionable tools so speech progress continues smoothly at home, in preschool, and in everyday life.
