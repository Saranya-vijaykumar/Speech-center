How It Works

Three gentle steps to clear communication

We remove the friction, anxiety, and clinical coldness from therapy.

1

Free 15-Minute Intake Consult

Speak directly with our intake clinical director. We listen to your family's concerns, developmental history, and answer all questions without pressure.

Schedule intake call →
2

Play-Based Assessment

A relaxed 45-minute evaluation in our sensory-friendly playroom or via telepractice. We identify oral motor, expressive, and receptive strengths.

View assessment roadmap →
3

Tailored Weekly Growth

Weekly 1-on-1 sessions paired with 5-minute home micro-games and parent debriefs, helping your child build confidence that lasts a lifetime.

Explore care plans →
