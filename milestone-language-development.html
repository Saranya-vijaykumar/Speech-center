2 – 5 Years: Vocabulary, Sentences & Clarity

The preschool window is where children transition from short phrases into complete grammatical storytelling, sound clarity (/r/, /s/, /l/), and peer conversational play.

Age 3

3-Word Sentences

Uses 200+ words, asks "what?" and "where?", understood 75% of the time by strangers.

Age 4

Complex Storytelling

Explains events of their day, uses past tense ("jumped"), masters /p/, /b/, /m/, /t/, /d/ sounds.

Age 5

Kindergarten Fluency

Speaks in complete sentences without stumbling, engages in conversational back-and-forth.
