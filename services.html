How We Can Help

1. Early Language & Delay

Life is challenging and overwhelming when speech development feels stalled. Together, we will work on strategies addressing late talking, first words, and toddler vocabulary growth. Each session is tailored to your child's unique pace and joy.

2. Articulation & Sounds

Unclear speech and sound substitutions can make communicating with peers and teachers frustrating. We employ tactile placement cues and gentle acoustic feedback to guide precise tongue and lip movement for crystal clear pronunciation.

3. Play & Fluency Therapy

A natural therapy session allows children to process thoughts, reduce stuttering tension, and express feelings through responsive play and assistive AAC technology. When given a safe space, children feel comfortable expressing their full authentic voice.

More About Services

Together, we can start your
journey towards communication.

The therapy process is unique to each person. Therefore, we utilize multiple evidence-based clinical strategies and a wide range of play-based techniques to accommodate your child's specific sensory and speech needs.

Whether you are experiencing the impact of delayed milestones, stuttering anxiety, low confidence, or difficulty expressing thoughts, we offer a warm, safe space where children and adults can communicate in an empathetic and neutral environment.

You will learn practical home tools and gain clinical resources to help your child thrive beyond our therapy rooms.

About Dr. Elena

I am a Licensed Speech-Language Pathologist (CCC-SLP) and Clinical Director with over 14 years of specialized pediatric and neurogenic communication experience.

On my days off you can find me outside exploring nature trails, reading developmental linguistics, and spending time with family. I believe being heard, understood, and supported is one of the most vital rights in the world.

What to Expect

Every therapy session looks a little different as the healing and communication process is unique to each individual. If you're new to speech therapy, here is a glimpse into the gentle process of what to expect when working with us:

1

Intake & Exploration

A relaxed 15-minute phone call to understand your child's background, routines, and specific developmental goals.

2

Comprehensive Play Evaluation

A 60-minute session assessing receptive language, speech phonetics, oral motor coordination, and social pragmatics.

3

Individualized Growth Plan

Weekly 45-minute sessions accompanied by parent coaching videos, flashcards, and celebratory milestone updates.
