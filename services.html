How We Can Help

The Clinical Flow

What to Expect

Every therapy session looks a little different as the healing and communication process is unique to each individual. Here is what to expect when partnering with our clinical team:

1

Intake & Exploration

A relaxed 15-minute phone call to understand your child's background, daily routines, and specific developmental goals.

✓ Free Consultation
2

Play Evaluation

A 60-minute session assessing receptive language, speech phonetics, oral motor coordination, and social pragmatics.

✓ Standardized Report
3

Weekly Growth Plan

Weekly 45-minute sessions accompanied by parent coaching videos, flashcards, and celebratory milestone updates.

✓ Ongoing Carryover
