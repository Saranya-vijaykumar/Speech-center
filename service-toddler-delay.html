Evidence-Based Clinical Protocol

Our Early Intervention Late Talker program empowers parents with naturalistic strategies to turn daily routines (mealtime, bath time, reading) into rich conversational opportunities. We target responsive joint attention, pointing gestures, vocal imitation, and expressive vocabulary acquisition.

Diagnostic Checklist

When Is It Time for an Evaluation?

Common indicators that suggest a specialized speech & language evaluation will be beneficial.

01

Fewer than 50 Words at Age 2

Toddler has a limited vocabulary and does not combine two words together spontaneously.

02

Lack of Pointing Gestures

Not using index finger pointing to show interest or request desired items.

03

Meltdowns Due to Frustration

Frequent tantrums when the child cannot convey basic needs to parents.

04

Reduced Joint Attention

Difficulty making shared eye contact during peekaboo or interactive toy games.

Step-by-Step Care Journey

Your Treatment Roadmap

From initial diagnostic assessment to conversational mastery and discharge.

01

Comprehensive Play Evaluation

We assess receptive language, expressive vocabulary, and gestural communication in a relaxed living-room environment.

02

Personalized Hanen Goal Plan

We define functional, meaningful words (names, verbs, requests) based on your child's favorite toys and routines.

03

Parent-Coached Play Sessions

Therapists guide parents in real time with child-led play techniques: waiting, imitating, and expanding words.

04

Conversational Independence

Child transitions to multi-word phrases and spontaneous conversational turn-taking.
