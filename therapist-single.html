Dr. Elena Vance is a board-certified Speech-Language Pathologist and Founding Clinical Director at SpeakBloom. With over 16 years of pediatric and neurological clinical experience, Dr. Vance specializes in toddler speech delay, Gestalt Language Processing (GLP), childhood apraxia of speech (PROMPT trained), and parent-empowerment coaching.

Clinical Philosophy & Approach

Communication is a fundamental human right. At SpeakBloom, we believe therapy should be child-centered, trauma-informed, and joyfully integrated into daily play and routines.

We collaborate intimately with parents and caregivers, turning home mealtime, playground trips, and bedtime stories into natural communication rich environments.

Specialized Areas of Focus

  • Early Childhood Language Delay (0–3 Years)
  • Gestalt Language Processing & Sensory Play
  • Childhood Apraxia of Speech (CAS & PROMPT)
  • Parent-Implemented Play Coaching
