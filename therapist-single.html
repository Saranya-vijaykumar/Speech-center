Clinical Case Study

12-Week Milestone Breakthrough

Aarav arrived at age 2.8 with severe expressive frustration and high anxiety around speech attempts. Using sensory play therapy, acoustic sound games, and Hanen 'More Than Words' parent coaching, Aarav expanded his vocabulary from 3 words to over 110 expressive phrases in under 3 months.
