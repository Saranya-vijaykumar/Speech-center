Insurance & Reimbursement

How Superbills & Insurance Reimbursement Work

SpeakBloom is an out-of-network private clinic, allowing our therapists to provide unconstrained, personalized care without restrictive insurance caps.

Following each therapy session, we provide an itemized medical Superbill featuring exact ICD-10 diagnostic codes and CPT procedure codes (e.g. 92507, 92523) for direct submission to your health insurer for 50% to 80% out-of-network reimbursement.

3 Simple Steps for Insurance:

1

Attend your scheduled 1-on-1 speech therapy session.

2

Receive your monthly itemized Superbill with diagnostic CPT codes automatically.

3

Submit to your insurer online or through HSA/FSA for direct reimbursement.
