Evidence-Based Clinical Protocol

Our Autism & AAC clinic provides neurodiversity-affirming care that honors echolalia as meaningful communication. We guide Gestalt Language Processors through Natural Language Acquisition (NLA) stages and configure high-tech augmentative devices.

Diagnostic Checklist

When Is It Time for an Evaluation?

Common indicators that suggest a specialized speech & language evaluation will be beneficial.

01

Gestalt Scripting (Echolalia)

Repeating lines from videos, songs, or conversations with rich melodic intonation.

02

Non-Speaking or Minimal Words

Child desires to communicate but struggles with oral motor planning and speech formulation.

03

Sensory Overload Meltdowns

Communication breaks down in noisy, overly stimulating classroom environments.

04

Difficulty with Single Words

Able to say full long phrases from TV shows but cannot answer simple "yes/no" or single-word questions.

Step-by-Step Care Journey

Your Treatment Roadmap

From initial diagnostic assessment to conversational mastery and discharge.

01

Sensory & NLA Stage Assessment

We evaluate the child's sensory profile, communication modalities, and Gestalt Language stage.

02

AAC Device Trial & Customization

Trial leading AAC apps (TouchChat, Proloquo2Go, LAMP) to find the child's intuitive motor navigation.

03

Mitigation & Grammar Expansion

Break down scripts into smaller chunks and support the creation of original spontaneous sentences.

04

Multi-Environment Communication

Equip families and teachers with device modeling strategies for home and school success.
