Evidence-Based Clinical Protocol

Our social communication groups help children and teens navigate peer relationships, understand conversational reciprocity, read subtle facial expressions, manage competitive games gracefully, and advocate for their authentic needs.

Diagnostic Checklist

When Is It Time for an Evaluation?

Common indicators that suggest a specialized speech & language evaluation will be beneficial.

01

Difficulty Initiating Conversations

Struggles to join peer games on the playground or say hello to classmates.

02

Dominating Topics of Interest

Talking exclusively about special interests without pausing for the listener's input.

03

Misinterpreting Sarcasm & Jokes

Taking figurative language, idioms, or light teasing very literally.

04

Meltdowns During Board Games

Overwhelming distress when rules change or when losing a competitive game.

Step-by-Step Care Journey

Your Treatment Roadmap

From initial diagnostic assessment to conversational mastery and discharge.

01

Social Cognitive Profile

Assessment of conversational turn-taking, theory of mind, and peer problem-solving abilities.

02

Direct Skill Coaching

Role-play active listening, topic maintenance, and conversational repair strategies in 1-on-1 therapy.

03

Matched Peer Group Play

Join a carefully matched 3-4 child cohort for collaborative games, LEGO building, and project work.

04

Community & School Generalization

Support friendships in real-world clubs, recess play, and birthday celebrations.
